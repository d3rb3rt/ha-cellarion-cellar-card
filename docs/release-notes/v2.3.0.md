## What's new in 2.3.0

### Added
- **Several cellars:** with more than one cellar, the cellar name at the top becomes a pulldown. Pick one cellar or *All cellars* together; in the combined view every place carries a small cellar tag. The choice is remembered per device. Settings and filters are shared by all cellars.
- **Choose the cellars in the setup and under Configure:** a multi-select with names. Leave it empty to show all of your own cellars; the first one chosen is the start cellar. Cellars shared with you are only included when you pick them there. Existing setups keep showing their cellar.

### Changed
- Filters that have nothing to choose right now stay in place, greyed out, instead of disappearing.
- The *Selected* line in a filter's pick list has an x to clear that filter, and the list stays open.
- Bottle positions in the rack map are larger on phones and a little smaller on tablets.
