## What's new in 2.4.0

### Added
- **Filter mode:** a new pulldown next to the filters switches between Default (as before), OR and NOT, with multi-select in every filter. OR shows bottles matching any chosen value (green, "or" between the chosen filters). NOT hides bottles matching any chosen value (red, "not" prefix), and the other filters follow the exclusions.
- **Three states per filter:** in the filter settings every filter, including the filter mode, can be On, behind "..." or Off.

### Changed
- The cellar pulldown is sorted alphabetically, lists *All cellars* first and starts with it; the pill is tinted and as tall as the search field.
- On phones the list shows only the rack name as the storage place.
- The statistics leave out Other items (not wine) when the list hides them, so bottle counts match.
