## What's new in 2.5.0

### Drunk wines: the new Archive
- **Archive switch:** a new switch in the filter bar shows every wine that has no bottle left, in violet, with the same information as your stock rows (region, appellation, classification, price, vintage, drunk count).
- **Detail view:** a bar lists the other vintages of the wine (violet when fully drunk). The drunk mark opens a small overlay with the date and notes of the drunk bottles. Identical entries are merged into one line with a count; the newest three are shown, the rest sits behind a button.
- **Search:** if a text search finds few stock hits, matching archive wines are added. The new *Archive* section in the settings sets how few ("only when nothing is found" by default).
- **One request instead of many:** the archive is now read together with the stock from Cellarion's inventory endpoint (`GET /api/inventory?include=consumed`). Drunk, gifted and sold bottles arrive in the same format as the stock, with wine facts and your personal fields included. This replaces the consumption log plus extra lookups per wine, so reloading is faster and puts less load on your server.
  **Requires Cellarion 1.294 or newer.** Older servers answer with an error for the new parameter; the card then falls back to showing the stock only.

### Statistics
- Tabs **Total**, **Stock** and **Drunk**. Every tile opens a drill-down (value, type, wines, producers, countries, vintages).
- New: wines and producers with nothing left in stock, and a list of your most drunk wines that are gone.

### Data fields
- **List and details are set separately:** each field can be shown in the list, in the details, in both, or switched off, with its own selection and order for each.
- **Every detail row is configurable:** all data (Cellarion fields, your personal fields and bottle data) is shown as one block of rows that you can sort, hide behind an expand arrow, or switch off.
- **Info texts:** a personal field `info:<attribute>` (e.g. `info:producer`, `info:region`, or your own field by its display name) makes the value of that data field tappable and shows its text in an overlay. The order of the info tabs follows the order of the data fields in the settings.
- **Country** is now a filter and a data field for the list and the details.
- New installations start with sensible defaults for filters, list info and detail rows.

### Also new
- **Price bands** setting (empty = price filter off); prices use the currency of your bottles.
- Sort option *Bottles (most first)*.
- Settings sheet with a fixed header and navigation pills for the groups.

### Changed
- Wine types and classification kinds come from your data instead of a built-in list; rack columns are used only when Cellarion states them.
- Search matches at the start of a word and looks for the whole phrase first; Enter opens the only remaining hit.
- Compact detail header; the vineyard classification row is labelled *Lagenstufe*.

### Fixed
- Android: the keyboard no longer pops up when scrolling after a search; lists and sheets end above the 3-button navigation bar.
- Filters behind "…" appear at their place in the filter order; the reset button also resets the OR/NOT mode.
